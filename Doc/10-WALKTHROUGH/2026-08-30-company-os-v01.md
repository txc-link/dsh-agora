# Company OS v0.1 walkthrough

## Outcome

Agora now has a durable company root independent of projects, formal units,
positions, employment history, an executive-assistant inbox, executable
assigned tasks, content-addressed Markdown deliverables, and a commitment
ledger. Matrix remains a replaceable adapter.

Implementation: `469a23b` (`master`). Matrix implementation: `0815aff`
(`txc-link/dsh-matrix-connector` `main`).

## Core model

- `organizations`: owner, purpose, immutable information-domain boundary.
- `organization_units`: executive office / department / team hierarchy.
- `organization_positions`: responsibilities, skills, and reporting line.
- `organization_employments`: human/agent subject ref, resident/on-demand/
  advisor kind, active/suspended/ended history.
- `executive_requests`: durable intake and routing state.
- `commitments`: task-backed owner promise with due date and evidence refs.
- `runtime_node_dispatches`: fenced work item consumed by the employed DSH
  runtime; completion advances the Company task only when the dispatch carries
  the explicit EA auto-advance marker.
- `artifacts`: SHA-256 content-addressed Markdown deliverable owned by the task
  and tagged with request, organization, dispatch, and information domain.

Organization is not Project. Existing project Team remains the execution-team
concept. Life, Health, and Companion are separate root security domains and
were not inserted as Company units.

## Entry surfaces

CLI:

```text
agora company create|list|show|unit|position|employment
agora assistant ask|inbox|commitments|show|reconcile
```

Matrix 0.3.0:

```text
/agora company
/agora company list
/agora assistant ask --capability research <request>
/agora assistant inbox [status]
/agora assistant commitments
/agora assistant show <request-id>
/agora assistant reconcile <request-id> [evidence-ref...]
```

`companyOrganization: austin-agent-company` is configured on node-b, so the
normal owner path does not require an organization id in every message.
